    The drawing runs like water

    Quick through the wave, pooling where it settles, thin on the way back.

    MechanismAn SVG of at most about ninety lines behind parts/Cycle. Each step has a pace; the same motes pass every point, so they thin out where it is fast and pool where it is slow. A fast mote is a short streak, a slow one a dot. One frame loop, only while the page is seen.

    The water runs on from where I am

    Drag the dot: a ring goes out where it lands, and what is behind me fades.

    How this is built
    MechanismWith the dot on a step, the water behind it fades to 20% and runs on from the dot. A landing on a detent sends one ring out over twice --t-page and pushes the nearby water on; the tick is unchanged. The dot is drawn where it was let go; the write follows.

    Holding the middle slows the water

    Each breath out, the whole stream runs a little slower.

    How this is built
    MechanismWhile a thumb holds the middle (breath.held), the stream gathers on the in-breath and lets go on the out-breath, and its calm falls a little each breath, never below 45%, coming back slowly after the thumb lifts.

    In Still it thins to embers

    Fewer, slower, dimmer; with Reduce Motion on, none at all.

    How this is built
    MechanismThe layer reads --stream when the light changes, never per frame: Still runs at .4 of the speed with fewer motes. Under Reduce Motion every motion token is zero, so there is no stream, no turn and no flight, and every tap still works.
