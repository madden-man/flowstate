    One thing at a time

    The first thing to do sits under the drawing, ahead of anything the page has to say.

    How this is built
    Stripui/flow/Tips.tsx: the step's things as cards side by side in a scroll-snap strip (.fl-deck) that follows the finger and lands on one. Every step, not only the peak.
    WherewalkCards splits cards from wise lines; the marks under the strip are drawn, never a number, and leave in Still.
