    Be opens on who I am

    My own line in ink; a paradox I have not written yet shows its three words in grey. Example data, live.

    How this is built
    Creedui/be/Who.tsx above the list in ui/be/Be.tsx: one line per held paradox, in list order, from who() in model/paradox.ts (the creed, else threeWords; own sets ink).
    DataParadox.creed, optional and sealed (db/fields.ts); no Dexie version bump.
    WhySelf-concept clarity is a few beliefs held confidently and stably, in his words (Campbell 1996). PLAN-SELF.md 2, 3.3.

    DemoThe real app, one classic script (design/be/src), storage in memory, no network, "today" pinned to 11 / 10. Each step loads it afresh, so it starts from its own state.

    A line opens its spread

    Tapping my line for selfless and selfish opens that spread below it.

    How this is built
    TapWho's onLine is toLine in Be.tsx: it flips the spread open if it is shut and seeks its row once laid out, as the #/be/:id deep link does.

    I tap the word to change it

    Selfish opens the paradox sheet with the caret already in its field.

    How this is built
    Wordsui/parts/Spread.tsx: the poles and the middle are .fs-poleword buttons calling onEdit('poleA' | 'poleB' | 'middle'); "Edit" stays.
    SheetopenParadox({ paradoxId, focus }) (ui/be/state.ts); ui/be/ParadoxSheet.tsx puts the caret at the end of that field.
    SavesaveParadox: the old words go onto revisions, struck through. It saves on the two words; the aspects may wait.

    Both, in my own sentence

    One line for holding the two together, written in the sheet and kept as I leave.

    How this is built
    Field"Both" in ParadoxSheet.tsx, placeholder "Both, in a sentence". Saved plainly, never a revision (sameCreed, withCreed); emptied, it goes.
    WhyWriting why a value matters, in one's own words, lowers defensiveness (Cohen and Sherman 2014); the app suggests nothing.

    Not me, under the spread

    What I refuse for this paradox sits after its goals and before today's checks.

    How this is built
    Linesui/be/NotMe.tsx between the spread and Due in Be.tsx: Paradox.refusals, one ink line each on its rule. No cadence, no check, no count.
    WhyA feared self works beside a wanted one in the same domain, concrete and about behaviour (Oyserman and Markus 1990; Ogilvie 1987). PLAN-SELF.md 3.4.

    One more refusal

    + Not me opens a line where it stands; at three the word goes.

    How this is built
    WriteaddRefusal(paradoxId, text), at most REFUSALS (3); a tap on a line edits it in place through saveRefusal, and an emptied line is removed.
    FieldThe council's edit line: Return or leaving keeps it, Escape puts it back; useKeyboardFit keeps it above the keyboard.
    RuleDESIGN.md 5, 6

    Five are held

    A sixth paradox is saved resting until one of the five rests.

    How this is built
    CapHELD = 5 and canHold in model/paradox.ts; createParadox lands resting past it, saveParadox throws HeldError, and the sheet's "active" option is inert.
    WhyCard sorts narrow to a top five to ten; more facets is not better (Miller 2001; Rafaeli-Mor and Steinberg 2002).

    A goal goes entirely

    Delete, then Delete it on the same row; its balance keeps a dashed slot.

    How this is built
    Sheetui/be/GoalSheet.tsx: the Delete row arms for four seconds, the second tap calls deleteGoal(id).
    WriteOne store.many(writes, drops): the row dropped, its balance's pairId and any nextTo on it cleared. Kept nowhere.

    A paradox goes entirely

    Two taps on Delete, and thoughtful and reactive leaves the list and the creed.

    How this is built
    SheetParadoxSheet.tsx ends on the same two-tap Delete row.
    WritedeleteParadox(id), one transaction: the paradox with its creed and refusals, its goals, its leans, and every page's link to it. Resting and retired stay the reversible ways down.
