    DoorThe word opens Right now and the flow pages read-only: the ring, the dot, the said lines and the seeded tips. A flow's words are sealed, so before the PIN they read from the seed (model/door.ts doorRead): his rewrites wait behind the PIN with kept lines, notes, "last time", the ghost rings, the journal link and Edit.
    PINThe moon or the Flow word raises the lock's own keypad in a sheet; the PIN writes the step he is on as the episode's mark, then the moon settles into Still and Flow opens the list.
    Back TapA Settings row explains the two-tap route: an iOS Shortcut that opens https://flowstate-journal.netlify.app/#/flow/now, set on Back Tap or the Action button. No code beyond the row.
    Route#/flow/now and #/flow/:id/:step are the only hashes the lock lets through (doorPasses), and only with settings.flowDoor on ("The ring without the PIN", off by default, shown once a PIN is set).

    How this is built
    Ringparts/Cycle, shape ring: one SVG, a drawn circle (rule 10: drawn paths), four marks, arrowheads clockwise, the arc from the last step back to the first dotted. Step words sit at its points; the shape is cycle/ring.ts behind one interface, so each later shape is one file.
    The dotWhere you are. At rest it sits on the dotted arc. It drags round the ring with a detent at each step (a tick, like the lean slider), and arrow keys walk it. It is a role="slider" from rest to the last step.
    EvidenceIt is a loop that closes: the literature confirms the order (build, peak, settle, linger) and the body's chemistry runs on a clock (PLAN-FLOWS.md 3.1).
    Ghost ringsEach earlier episode is a faint closed circle outside the live one, one ring further out each time, oldest outermost. Rule 15 bans counts; this is a drawing, and the page prints no number near it. Still hides them (open question 9). Package F6, not drawn here yet.
    Walk it onceAt rest, a tap on a step word only reads that step; the episode starts when the dot is dragged. So the ring can be rehearsed calm, which is when a skill is learned.
    DataAn episodes row starts when the dot first leaves rest: { flowId, startedAt, marks: [{ stepId, at }] }. Nothing is counted; the row is a clock.

    How this is built
    BreathThe dot's halo runs on --t-breath (4s in, 6s out: six a minute, the rate of the slow-breathing studies). Same animation as Still's ring; no new token. The iPhone ticks at the turn points (haptic pacing was preferred 19 of 24, Paredes 2018).
    HoldA thumb on the middle of the ring runs the breath (flow/breath.ts): the halo scales under it, the word reads in or out, and each turn ticks through parts/Haptic (the breath site, rule 19). The first tick is inside the touch; the later ones come from a timer under the held finger, and whether the iPhone feels them is still to be checked on the phone.
    One thingAt the peak the tips show one at a time, full width in the title size; a swipe brings the next. Acute stress narrows attention (PLAN-FLOWS.md 3.1, P25, P29), so a list becomes a single instruction.
    Toneparts/Sound plays a rising tone on the in-breath and a falling one on the out, so the phone can go face down and the breath still has a pacer. Its own Breath tone switch sits under Sounds, off by default, and is silent whenever Sounds is off (rule 21).
    AwakeThe Wake Lock API holds the screen while the dot is on this step and the page is seen, released when it moves or the page hides; where the API is missing, nothing happens. A ring that goes dark mid-breath is a small cruelty.
    One thingThe said line no longer repeats the numbers "Usually" carries (the F1 review). Attacks peak within minutes and most last 5 to 20 (NHS); adrenaline's half-life is about 3.5 minutes.
    NeverA paper bag (it lowers oxygen; Callaham 1989). The two-inhale sigh is daily practice evidence, not a rescue, so it is not seeded here.
