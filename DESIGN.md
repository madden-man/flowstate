# FlowState design standard

The look and feel of FlowState, written so it can be checked. Every rule is marked **lint** (checked by `node script/design-lint.mjs`, which must print PASS before any PR) or **review** (checked by the reviewer against the walkthrough). The live reference is `design/walkthrough.html` with `design/tokens.css`; package 0 ships that tokens file unchanged as `src/ui/tokens.css`. The notebook "Who to Be, How to Flow" is the spec behind all of it.

## 1. What the design is for

The notebook names three needs. The app answers each with a light, and everything else follows from that.

| Need, in the notebook's words | Light | When | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| "an enormous amount of potential energy... wish to use it in all seriousness" | **Day** | sunrise to sunset | cream page, blue rules, crisp ink; the checks under a flow's step |
| "my supreme duty to listen to the quiet voices within" | **Night** | sunset to sunrise | deep page, chalk ink, a lamp in the corner, voices glowing |
| "boiling in my own soup... wait out the truly hard parts, they never last forever" | **Still** | chosen, never automatic | deepest page, soft contrast, slower, counts gone, one kept line |

Mystical means wordless and incomplete here ("wisdom is to be experienced, and it is always incomplete"), so the app makes room rather than content: the page follows the real sky, the threshold is a breath, your own lines return as the echo, and nothing is ever generated for you.

## 2. Tokens (lint)

1. Every colour, font, size, spacing and duration in the UI comes from `tokens.css` as a `var(--…)`. No colour literal (`#hex`, `rgb()`, `hsl()`, `oklch()` with numbers), no `font-family`, no `transition`/`animation` duration literal anywhere else.
2. Three lights, one attribute: `data-light="day|night|still"` on `<html>`. A component never checks the light in code to pick a colour; it uses the token and the token changes.
3. Voices are hues, not swatches. A voice stores a hue angle; its colour is `oklch(var(--voice-l) var(--voice-c) <hue>)`, so lightness and chroma come from the light. Deep twins: lightness −17%, chroma × .6.
4. Fonts are the phone's own: `ui-serif` for ink (New York on iPhone), `ui-sans-serif` for printed stationery labels. Nothing is downloaded; no `@import`, no `<link href="http…">`, no `url(http…)`.

## 3. The page (review)

5. Every writing surface is a page: the notebook header ("No." and "DATE: 11 / 7 / 26" in grey sans, top right), rules the text sits on (line height equals rule spacing), ink in serif. Day 17 on 28; Still 18 on 33. A row that can wrap (a check, a question) ends on a blank rule however many lines its text took, so one line is text plus a blank rule and three lines is text plus a blank rule; dense indexes (Pages, councils) stay one line each, cut with an ellipsis.
6. One primary act per screen, and it is the first thing under the thumb. Journal opens on a blank dated page with the caret ready. No save buttons; everything autosaves.
7. Tabs are four words (Journal, Council, Be, Flow) and, third, the **?** mark for Questions (V2): a glyph in the serif a size up, like the header's ♪, not a fifth word. No icons anywhere except the moon, the ring, the drawn arrow, the drawn check and the mountain's seven signs (a crown, an eye, a speech bubble, a heart, a sun, a drop, a sprout; rule 34), which are drawn paths in the app's own code (`parts/Sign`), never an icon library or an emoji. No icon libraries (lint).
8. Sheets rise; nothing is a modal dialog (`<dialog>`, `role="dialog"`, `alert`, `confirm`, `prompt` are banned, lint). Back is a swipe. Long press means pick up, keep close or reply.
9. A voice has a role (Architect, King) and may have a name Tommy gives it (V10); the name is what shows where it speaks, and a tap on the name above a line shows the role beside it. Messages spoken by a voice carry a 3px left rule in the voice colour and a 10% wash (0% in Still); deep twins carry a doubled rule. Chips: starred voices first, "+ voice" last. A line confirmed as spoken for a value prints the word after the speaker, in print ("WARRIOR · FOR HONESTY"; PLAN-VALUES.md C4): confirmed after the line is spoken, by a tap on the line, never armed before it; the app prints the label, never the sentence.
10. Revisions are shown struck through with a drawn stroke, never hidden; corner tags are a line across the corner with the value written along it; arrows between items are drawn paths.
11. Be is a list of values in tiers under the notebook's line "I determine my core." (PLAN-VALUES.md, which retires the spread, its goals, their pairs, the dashed balance slots and the leans; PLAN-TIERS.md, which retires the one flat list): the headings First, Second and Third, each over a row per value it holds, the word large, its champions' hues as short `.fs-spoke` rules beside it; then the words Resting and Legacy, each a shelf that opens. The tier is said by its heading and the word's size (First `--fs-manifesto`, Second `--fs-title`, Third and shelf rows `--fs-ink`), never a number, a colour, a badge or a count (rule 15). A Resting row is undated; a Legacy row carries the dates it was held where the rules would be. A dashed landing rule marks somewhere to put a value: an empty tier always shows one; while a value is lifted every tier ends in one and Resting and Legacy each open with one. A tap opens the value where it stands: its word travels left, the seam is drawn and its shadow is written across it, and under that row grow the looks line, the champions of each side, what was said for it and "Call a council". A shadow is a value too and is drawn as one: italic is its only mark, never greyed, never doubled, never a word for lesser. A champion is drawn as that voice's line (its 3px rule and wash) holding his why. A value nobody fights for carries a dashed rule on the list and, inside, the dashed line "who fights for this?", the mark for something owed. Nothing is ever laid out while hidden: measure after display, and relayout on resize (the open value's `Fold` measures its height once drawn).

## 4. The lights (review)

12. The light follows the sky: sunrise and sunset computed offline from a stored place (one-time in Settings; fallback 07:00 to 19:00); sunrise and sunset change the light in one `--t-light` breath, everything together (V5). Settings can pin Day or Night. The moon glows after sunset but never switches Still on by itself.
13. Still is a tap on the moon, from anywhere (V6: pulling the page down shows earlier pages instead, and does nothing in Still). In Still: no counts, states, page numbers or page list (today only); Be is the core line and First's words alone, at one size (every held word, in tier order, when First is empty; PLAN-TIERS.md section 6), with no segment, no headings, no shadows, no champions, no whys, no said-for lines, nothing that opens or lifts (PLAN-VALUES.md step 11); deep twins leave the chip row; a breathing ring (4s in, 6s out) replaces the moon and is the exit; one kept line sits at the top; after twelve minutes of typing the ring asks once, "Enough for now?"; Meditate is the circle and the sound chips, a sit begun there is open (no length, no ink line, no closing bell) and nothing about the last sit shows, though the sit is still kept; Energize's three (PLAN-ENERGIZE.md) are their names only, with no since line, no stroke under them, no deck and no What stuck, and the water runs in ink. Leaving Still (ring, or the next sunrise) changes no data.
14. Still lowers luminance, not warmth: page #0F1016, ink #A19B8E (about 6.5:1), chroma × .4, cool-neutral. (Illuminance moves arousal; colour temperature barely does.)
15. Nothing counts a streak. No badges, confetti, counts on tabs (lint on the words in `src/ui`). A drawing of past episodes is not a count (F6): the ghost rings behind a flow's drawing and his own stamps on the What if curve print no number anywhere near them, are capped so the page never fills, and leave in Still. The stream on a flow's drawing (rule 30) is not a count either: it stands for no number of anything. A sit keeps its date and its sound ("Last sat · 11 / 8 · ocean"), never a total, a count or a run of days. A fixation's span (PLAN-ENERGIZE.md) is a duration in words, like a flow's "last time" ("Since 11 / 7 · 4 days"), not a streak: it counts no days done, has no day to miss and nothing to break, and it leaves in Still. The ribbons on What stuck are a drawing of when each one was on, longest held first; they print no number, no total and no rank, only the dates in the print lines beside the names, and they leave in Still with the dates, so the page is the names alone. "Said for it" under a value (PLAN-VALUES.md C5) is a list of his lines with their dates, never a number, a total or a rank; "Earlier" shows the rest in place.

## 5. Your own words (review)

16. Kept lines: long press a message, "Keep close". The echo above a new page is a kept line, oldest unseen first ("Same answer as last"); tap to link, swipe away. Still shows kept lines only; the seed is the manifesto. The app never writes a line for you.
17. Turn to answer: one tap makes an entry a question and opens the answer frame beneath, set in italic as the wiser second person. (Distanced self-talk lowers rumination over time; the notebook already answers "stand tall, young one".)

## 6. Motion, touch, sound (lint where marked)

18. Durations: `--t-ui` 180ms, `--t-page` 400ms, `--t-draw` 320ms (a pen stroke), `--t-hold` 500ms (a long press filling), `--t-light` 1200ms, `--t-breath` 10s, `--t-orbit` 9s. Easing `--ease`, and `--ease-settle` for a thing that travels and lands; never a bounce or spring, never an overshoot. Any file with `animation:` contains a `prefers-reduced-motion` rule (lint); all durations drop to zero there, and `--stream` with them.
19. Haptics only through `parts/Haptic` (the hidden `<input type="checkbox" switch>` trick; `navigator.vibrate` is banned, lint). Ticks on: unlock, tab change, a check, an item dropped (`drop`), a lifted value on Be crossing into another place, a tier or a shelf (`tier`, `ui/be/Be.tsx`, PLAN-TIERS.md B2; it comes under a held finger, so on the iPhone it may come to nothing), the dot's detent at a flow's step (`parts/Cycle`, F1, and Spark it putting the dot on Energize's ride, the same site through its `detentTick`, PLAN-ENERGIZE.md X5), entering Still, keep close, a sheet opening, a cover chosen on the music sheet or the On sheet (`album`, M3; PLAN-PICKS.md P1), and the ring's turn points while a thumb holds the middle of a flow's drawing (`breath`, F2; the turns after the first come from a timer under the held finger, so on the iPhone they may come to nothing), the tap on Meditate's circle that begins or ends a sit (`sit`, PLAN-MEDITATE.md S1), on Energize a fixation put in a slot from the deck (`swap`) and Deal me one (`deal`, PLAN-ENERGIZE.md X3), on Be a voice taking up or leaving a value in the champion sheets (`champion`) and a line confirmed as spoken for one (`for`, PLAN-VALUES.md C3 and C4), and on the mountain the Depression meter (`meter`) and a gate (`gate`, on the mountain and on its sign's page) landing on either end, the two ends only, never along the way (PLAN-MOUNTAIN.md R2, R3). Nowhere else. Silent mode may mute them; the app degrades to nothing.
20. Tactility without haptics: a lifted value or voice rides under the finger to its new place, the rail of earlier pages follows the drag, long press lifts an item 2px (the goals and the lean slider left with the spread, PLAN-VALUES.md C6). Drag feedback is immediate (no transition on where a dragged thing is). On Be (PLAN-TIERS.md section 5) a lift folds every open value at once, so each row is one height; near the list's top or bottom edge the list scrolls under the row, by hand, faster nearer the edge; the riding word eases to its place's size over `--t-ui`, and on drop the row settles into its slot over `--t-ui` with `--ease-settle`.
21. Sound only through `parts/Sound` (Web Audio synthesis, no files; `new AudioContext` elsewhere is banned, lint): a bell on unlock, a long low tone on settling, a soft bell on keep close, a short bell on choosing an album, a rising fifth on putting a fixation in a slot (`swap`, G to D, the breath tone's two ends struck as two short soft bells; PLAN-ENERGIZE.md X3), a single very quiet short D as a lifted value crosses into another place on Be (`tier`, PLAN-TIERS.md B2), and the breath tone while a thumb holds a flow's ring (rising on the in-breath, falling on the out; F2), behind its own Breath tone switch under Sounds, off by default and silent whenever Sounds is off. Never uninvited; a Settings switch; follows the ring switch. The one exception is a sit (PLAN-MEDITATE.md): the four scapes (rain, the ocean, a forest, a bowl) are begun only by the tap on Meditate's circle, fade in over an in-breath and out over an out-breath, and are chosen by the chip under the circle (Quiet is their off, so they do not follow Sounds); alone among the app's sounds they are media, played with the ringer switch off while "Sit sounds with the ringer off" is on. A sit with a length is closed by one bell when its circle is drawn round (`sitEnd`, 528 Hz over four seconds, lower and longer than unlock); it is a bell, so it follows Sounds.

The motion pass (PLAN-MOTION.md) adds rules 26 to 31. The numbers run on from section 8 so no older rule is renumbered.

26. Press (review): down is instant, up is eased. `parts/Press` puts `data-down` on the control under the thumb at pointerdown; its down state has no transition, and it comes back over `--t-ui`. Nothing waits for the click to answer a touch.
27. Drawn, not shown (review): a mark that appears is drawn in the order a pen would draw it, over `--t-draw`: the check, the ring round the playing album, the mark under the tab. A mark that goes is lifted over `--t-ui`.
28. The same thing stays the same thing (review): when a thing on one screen is the same thing on the next, it travels there instead of vanishing and appearing: a tapped cover to the small cover by the ♪, a flow's small drawing to the drawing on its page, a playlist's strip to its grid. Travel uses `--ease-settle` and goes through `parts/Turn` (one view transition at a time), so where the browser has none, or Reduce Motion is on, the change just happens.
29. The paper stays, the ink turns (review): between pages, the header's left words, the rules and the tab bar do not move; only what is written moves, a short way, toward the side it came from. Still spreads from the moon in a circle and closes back into the ring; sunrise and sunset stay rule 12's one breath. Sitting is not a turn: it is the one place the paper itself leaves, the header, the rules, the tab bar and every word fading over `--t-light` until only Meditate's circle is left, and coming back the same way.
30. The stream (review): a flow's drawing runs like water, behind `parts/Cycle`. Each step has a pace; the same motes pass every point, so they thin out where it runs fast and pool where it runs slow. With the dot on a step, what is behind it fades; a ring goes out where the dot lands; a held thumb slows all of it on the out-breath. The water is ink, except on Energize (PLAN-ENERGIZE.md X2): there the channel runs as three lanes side by side, one a fixation, each in that fixation's hue in slot order (an empty slot's lane stays ink, and so does all of it in Still and through the door), and a fixation landing in its slot sends the ring out in its hue. A ride named by Spark it (X5) runs in one: while that episode is live every lane takes the ridden fixation's hue, on every step, and back at rest the three return; a ride dragged off rest names nothing and keeps the three. `--stream` scales it by the light (Day 1, Night .85, Still .4, Reduce Motion 0). It is a drawing, never a count (rule 15), and the fastest water stays slower than a finger. On the mountain (rule 34) the water is light on the night plate, not ink, in the line colour in Still.
31. Motion answers, it never asks (review): every animation starts from a touch or from the sky, runs once, and ends still, except the three that breathe (the ring, with Meditate's circle, which is the ring at the size of the page; the halo; the stream; the mountain's sea, swaying on the same breath) and the bead on the playing album. Nothing loops to draw the eye, nothing delays a tap, and no screen is unusable while something is moving.

PLAN-ENERGIZE.md adds rule 32, numbered on from the motion pass for the same reason.

32. Energize is the loud page (review): the one screen meant to wind him up, so it turns up what the standard already has and nothing more. Colour: each fixation's hue on its card (a voice's 3px rule and wash, rules 3 and 9) and in its lane of the water (rule 30). Size: the names at `--fs-manifesto`, the largest type in the app, each over a stroke drawn like a pen (rule 27). Speed: its water is already moving at rest (`model/pace.ts`). Bounce, spring and overshoot (rule 18), confetti, badges and streak words (rule 15), anything that loops or asks for attention (rule 31) and any generated word (rule 16) stay banned here as everywhere; every name on it is his.

PLAN-MOUNTAIN.md adds rule 34. The walk (branch `walk`, not yet merged here) takes 33, so the mountain is numbered after it and neither is renumbered.

34. The mountain (review; the names are lint): the Flow tab is one drawing, a night plate with the land in chalk line, seven signs down one stream and a sea where Meditate and Energize float. The plate is dark in every light (`--plate`, `--plate-sky`, `--plate-deep`, set per light, so no component checks the light, rule 2): by Day it is a print set inside the page's column with the cream round it (`--plate-inset`), by Night and in Still it runs to the screen's edges and feathers into the page (`--plate-feather`), and its box is the same size in every light so nothing moves at sunset. The water is its only light: no sun, no moon in the drawing; the rock is lit only as far as the water runs, and an open sign is a lantern that throws its hue a short way downstream. Nothing in the drawing stands for nothing: each place's weather (spindrift, the fall at the Throat, the tarn at the Heart, embers, the pool, fireflies, the river mouth) is the stream's light doing something there. The seven are named by their part of the body or soul, Crown, Third eye, Throat, Heart, Solar plexus, Sacral, Root ("Heart chakra" in its page's header), never a Sanskrit word, a lotus, a petal, an element shape, a seed letter or "Meru" (lint DL22). The meter and the gates are states he sets (rule 15): no number on the meter, a gate's grey percent only while it is above zero, each written once on release as a reading (never while dragging, never by a tap or a scroll), dashed when a day old, and hidden in Still with every hue, the snow line and the dark below a gate. A change travels down at the water's pace (about 5.5 seconds from spring to sea) instead of landing everywhere at once; a gate shut pools the water above it, one opened sends it down in one surge; Reduce Motion puts it there at once. Snow falls only while the meter rises, and the air is still again soon after (rule 31): a heavy day already set is a bright held cap and a dark mountain, not a loop. The arrival draws it once per unlock and any touch cuts it short; the sea's breath and the stream join the things that breathe. The Depression rule starts a thumb's width (`--hit`) in from the plate's edges, so its mark at rest is never under the phone's edge swipe. A sign's page shows its place and no other drawing: the old flows' diagrams left it (Tommy, 2026-11-11), a flow in front prints its step words on one line (`.ck-steps`), and the ghost rings, stamps, clock and hold the ring stay on a flow with no sign (Energize, one of his own).

PLAN-TASKS.md adds rule 35, numbered on from the mountain.

35. The tasks become the water (review): a sign's page is its place. The plate is the page's top, the sign's touch words printed on it beside the stream, and under it the paper foot: the gloss, the gate, the sign's shelf, the printed safety lines (the 988 line with Call and Text on Heart and Root); at rest there is no checklist. A task is a touch word, one or two print words with a thumb's hit area, never a checkbox: at most four a sign (`model/acts.ts`), the lead one lit at rest, a tap lighting it and another word putting it back. A touch does one of three things and nothing else: it happens in the place, in what the place is made of (a rule on the snow or the tarn, a held thumb on the breath, the lanterns in the firs); it carries his line into Journal, Council, Be, Questions or the sea, where the sign is printed by the date (on the header's top line, over it) and a tap on it comes back (rule 28); or it leaves the app by a real link (`sms:`, `tel:`, `calshow:`, Spotify) whose word is what it opens, the person's name or Calendar. Nothing is shown that the app cannot act on, and the app writes no words of its own into any of it (rule 16). A touch done moves water and never writes the gate: it lets a share of the pool above its sign go as one surge, the gate's handle goes dashed to ask until he sets it or leaves, a touch that left the app surges when he comes back, and the first return to the mountain runs it to the sea, once. No tallies: no count of touches, no streak, no "done today" (rule 15); Root's basics go out at sunrise. The shelf sits under the gate and holds its height from first paint: covers wordless, a tap plays, the plus raises a small sheet that writes a line (a book, a piece of art, a show, a film, a game; the mountain's R4), and a long press lifts a cover or a line to move or take off, ringed in ink while the rest dim. An album with no kept cover is an empty tile with its title on it in print, never a written row. Still: the touches work, with no hue, no pool, no surge, no plus and no lifting; Reduce Motion: a word and a mark light at once and nothing travels. Through the door only the touches that write nothing are printed, with no gate and no shelf.

## 7. Network (lint)

22. The app makes no network request after the first load. `fetch(` is allowed only under `src/music/` (the Spotify tie-in, opt-in, PKCE, no server). Covers are cached as blobs so the album grid works offline.

## 8. Process (review)

23. Every package PR carries its walkthrough step(s) in `design/walkthrough.html`, built from the real components and `tokens.css`, passing `node ~/.claude/hooks/artifact-lint.mjs`.
24. A new screen is reviewed in all three lights before merge (the walkthrough's relight control exists for this).
25. New tokens go into `tokens.css` with a comment naming the screen that needed them; a new colour needs a reason the existing ones do not cover.

## 9. Research behind the choices

- Calm technology (Amber Case): the smallest possible attention, use the periphery, work when it fails → the sky in the periphery, no notifications, offline always. [Calm principles as cited](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.05741), [Designing calm](https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2025/05/designing-calm-ux-principles-for-reducing-users-anxiety.php).
- Slow technology (Hallnäs and Redström; Odom et al. 2022: temporal interconnectedness, temporal drift, explicit slowness) → the page lit by the real sun, the breath, 400ms page turns. [Slow technology](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1007/PL00000019), [office installation study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.11772).
- Self-distancing (Kross and Ayduk): reflecting from a distanced perspective and non-first-person self-talk lowers rumination over time → Turn to answer, the wiser second person. [Chapter](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/emotion-selfcontrol-psych/wp-content/uploads/sites/1322/2017/09/Self-distancing-theory-research-future.pdf), [Kross 2011](https://rascl.studentorg.berkeley.edu/assets/files/kross_ayduk_2011_cd.pdf).
- Lighting and arousal: higher illuminance raises self-rated arousal, colour temperature does not significantly → Still dims rather than warms. [VR lighting study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.10969). Dark vs light reading: no comprehension difference, less glare in low light → Night by sky is safe. [Summary](https://android.gadgethacks.com/news/dark-mode-vs-light-mode-what-research-says-about-when-each-works-best/).
- Apple Journal reviews: restraint works, surfacing what already happened beats prompting, long prompts feel heavy under stress → no prompts, the echo, Still never asks. [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/07/17/how-to-make-apple-journal-part-of-a-mindful-daily-routine), [14-day test](https://blog.vividiary.live/reviews/ios-27-apple-journal-insights-tab-review).
- Web haptics on iPhone: `navigator.vibrate` never shipped in WebKit; the switch trick fires the Taptic Engine on a direct tap, iOS 17.4+, single tick from 26.5. [haptics library notes](https://haptics.kushagragolash.dev/), [state of web haptics 2026](https://creativealive.com/haptics-web-whats-possible-whats-fake-2026/).
- Spotify from a PWA: Web Playback SDK support on iOS Safari is contradictory in the field and stops in the background; the Web API player endpoints need Premium; development mode allows the owner plus a few users. [Grimoire issue](https://github.com/Larront/grimoire/issues/243), [community thread](https://community.spotify.com/t5/Spotify-for-Developers/Web-Playback-SDK-should-work-on-iOS-13-4-but-it-does-not/td-p/5232605).
