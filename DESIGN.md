# FlowState design standard

The look and feel of FlowState, written so it can be checked. Every rule is marked **lint** (checked by `node script/design-lint.mjs`, which must print PASS before any PR) or **review** (checked by the reviewer against the walkthrough). The live reference is `design/walkthrough.html` with `design/tokens.css`; package 0 ships that tokens file unchanged as `src/ui/tokens.css`. The notebook "Who to Be, How to Flow" is the spec behind all of it.

## 1. What the design is for

The notebook names three needs. The app answers each with a light, and everything else follows from that.

| Need, in the notebook's words | Light | When | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| "an enormous amount of potential energy... wish to use it in all seriousness" | **Day** | sunrise to sunset | cream page, blue rules, crisp ink; the checks under a spread on Be |
| "my supreme duty to listen to the quiet voices within" | **Night** | sunset to sunrise | deep page, chalk ink, a lamp in the corner, voices glowing |
| "boiling in my own soup... wait out the truly hard parts, they never last forever" | **Still** | chosen, never automatic | deepest page, soft contrast, slower, counts gone, one kept line |

Mystical means wordless and incomplete here ("wisdom is to be experienced, and it is always incomplete"), so the app makes room rather than content: the page follows the real sky, the threshold is a breath, your own lines return as the echo, and nothing is ever generated for you.

## 2. Tokens (lint)

1. Every colour, font, size, spacing and duration in the UI comes from `tokens.css` as a `var(--…)`. No colour literal (`#hex`, `rgb()`, `hsl()`, `oklch()` with numbers), no `font-family`, no `transition`/`animation` duration literal anywhere else.
2. Three lights, one attribute: `data-light="day|night|still"` on `<html>`. A component never checks the light in code to pick a colour; it uses the token and the token changes.
3. Voices are hues, not swatches. A voice stores a hue angle; its colour is `oklch(var(--voice-l) var(--voice-c) <hue>)`, so lightness and chroma come from the light. Deep twins: lightness −17%, chroma × .6.
4. Fonts are the phone's own: `ui-serif` for ink (New York on iPhone), `ui-sans-serif` for printed stationery labels. Nothing is downloaded; no `@import`, no `<link href="http…">`, no `url(http…)`.

## 3. The page (review)

5. Every writing surface is a page: the notebook header ("No." and "DATE: 11 / 7 / 26" in grey sans, top right), rules the text sits on (line height equals rule spacing), ink in serif. Day 17 on 28; Still 18 on 33. A row that can wrap (a check, a question) ends on a blank rule however many lines its text took, so one line is text plus a blank rule and three lines is text plus a blank rule; dense indexes (Pages, councils) stay one line each, cut with an ellipsis.
6. One primary act per screen, and it is the first thing under the thumb. Journal opens on a blank dated page with the caret ready. No save buttons; everything autosaves.
7. Tabs are four words (Journal, Council, Be, Flow) and, third, the **?** mark for Questions (V2): a glyph in the serif a size up, like the header's ♪, not a fifth word. No icons anywhere except the moon, the ring, the drawn arrow and the drawn check. No icon libraries (lint).
8. Sheets rise; nothing is a modal dialog (`<dialog>`, `role="dialog"`, `alert`, `confirm`, `prompt` are banned, lint). Back is a swipe. Long press means pick up, keep close or reply.
9. A voice has a role (Architect, King) and may have a name Tommy gives it (V10); the name is what shows where it speaks, and a tap on the name above a line shows the role beside it. Messages spoken by a voice carry a 3px left rule in the voice colour and a 10% wash (0% in Still); deep twins carry a doubled rule. Chips: starred voices first, "+ voice" last.
10. Revisions are shown struck through with a drawn stroke, never hidden; corner tags are a line across the corner with the value written along it; arrows between items are drawn paths.
11. The spread is two facing pages: pole A's goals on the left, pole B's on the right, each at the height of its lean. A goal is normally added with the goal that balances it on the other pole; the pair is linked across the seam by a dotted path, and a missing balance is a dashed slot at the same height that stays until filled. Nothing is ever laid out while hidden: measure after display, and relayout on resize. The Be tab is a list first (V8): a row per paradox, its two poles facing across a short seam, and a tap opens that paradox's spread under its row.

## 4. The lights (review)

12. The light follows the sky: sunrise and sunset computed offline from a stored place (one-time in Settings; fallback 07:00 to 19:00); sunrise and sunset change the light in one `--t-light` breath, everything together (V5). Settings can pin Day or Night. The moon glows after sunset but never switches Still on by itself.
13. Still is a tap on the moon, from anywhere (V6: pulling the page down shows earlier pages instead, and does nothing in Still). In Still: no counts, states (the checks and the lean under a spread on Be), page numbers or page list (today only); deep twins leave the chip row; a breathing ring (4s in, 6s out) replaces the moon and is the exit; one kept line sits at the top; after twelve minutes of typing the ring asks once, "Enough for now?"; Meditate is the circle and the sound chips, a sit begun there is open (no length, no ink line, no closing bell) and nothing about the last sit shows, though the sit is still kept; Energize's three (PLAN-ENERGIZE.md) are their names only, with no since line, no stroke under them, no deck and no What stuck, and the water runs in ink. Leaving Still (ring, or the next sunrise) changes no data.
14. Still lowers luminance, not warmth: page #0F1016, ink #A19B8E (about 6.5:1), chroma × .4, cool-neutral. (Illuminance moves arousal; colour temperature barely does.)
15. Nothing counts a streak. No badges, confetti, counts on tabs (lint on the words in `src/ui`). A drawing of past episodes is not a count (F6): the ghost rings behind a flow's drawing and his own stamps on the What if curve print no number anywhere near them, are capped so the page never fills, and leave in Still. The stream on a flow's drawing (rule 30) is not a count either: it stands for no number of anything. A sit keeps its date and its sound ("Last sat · 11 / 8 · ocean"), never a total, a count or a run of days. A fixation's span (PLAN-ENERGIZE.md) is a duration in words, like a flow's "last time" ("Since 11 / 7 · 4 days"), not a streak: it counts no days done, has no day to miss and nothing to break, and it leaves in Still. The ribbons on What stuck are a drawing of when each one was on, longest held first; they print no number, no total and no rank, only the dates in the print lines beside the names, and they leave in Still with the dates, so the page is the names alone.

## 5. Your own words (review)

16. Kept lines: long press a message, "Keep close". The echo above a new page is a kept line, oldest unseen first ("Same answer as last"); tap to link, swipe away. Still shows kept lines only; the seed is the manifesto. The app never writes a line for you.
17. Turn to answer: one tap makes an entry a question and opens the answer frame beneath, set in italic as the wiser second person. (Distanced self-talk lowers rumination over time; the notebook already answers "stand tall, young one".)

## 6. Motion, touch, sound (lint where marked)

18. Durations: `--t-ui` 180ms, `--t-page` 400ms, `--t-draw` 320ms (a pen stroke), `--t-hold` 500ms (a long press filling), `--t-light` 1200ms, `--t-breath` 10s, `--t-orbit` 9s. Easing `--ease`, and `--ease-settle` for a thing that travels and lands; never a bounce or spring, never an overshoot. Any file with `animation:` contains a `prefers-reduced-motion` rule (lint); all durations drop to zero there, and `--stream` with them.
19. Haptics only through `parts/Haptic` (the hidden `<input type="checkbox" switch>` trick; `navigator.vibrate` is banned, lint). Ticks on: unlock, tab change, a check, an item dropped, a lean detent, the dot's detent at a flow's step (`parts/Cycle`, F1), entering Still, keep close, a sheet opening, a cover chosen on the music sheet or the On sheet (`album`, M3; PLAN-PICKS.md P1), and the ring's turn points while a thumb holds the middle of a flow's drawing (`breath`, F2; the turns after the first come from a timer under the held finger, so on the iPhone they may come to nothing), the tap on Meditate's circle that begins or ends a sit (`sit`, PLAN-MEDITATE.md S1), and on Energize a fixation put in a slot from the deck (`swap`) and Deal me one (`deal`, PLAN-ENERGIZE.md X3). Nowhere else. Silent mode may mute them; the app degrades to nothing.
20. Tactility without haptics: goals slide up and down their page under the finger, the lean slider clicks into three detents, the rail of earlier pages follows the drag, long press lifts an item 2px. Drag feedback is immediate (no transition while dragging).
21. Sound only through `parts/Sound` (Web Audio synthesis, no files; `new AudioContext` elsewhere is banned, lint): a bell on unlock, a long low tone on settling, a soft bell on keep close, the lean's two ends, a short bell on choosing an album, a rising fifth on putting a fixation in a slot (`swap`, G to D, the breath tone's two ends struck as two short soft bells; PLAN-ENERGIZE.md X3), and the breath tone while a thumb holds a flow's ring (rising on the in-breath, falling on the out; F2), behind its own Breath tone switch under Sounds, off by default and silent whenever Sounds is off. Never uninvited; a Settings switch; follows the ring switch. The one exception is a sit (PLAN-MEDITATE.md): the four scapes (rain, the ocean, a forest, a bowl) are begun only by the tap on Meditate's circle, fade in over an in-breath and out over an out-breath, and are chosen by the chip under the circle (Quiet is their off, so they do not follow Sounds); alone among the app's sounds they are media, played with the ringer switch off while "Sit sounds with the ringer off" is on. A sit with a length is closed by one bell when its circle is drawn round (`sitEnd`, 528 Hz over four seconds, lower and longer than unlock); it is a bell, so it follows Sounds.

The motion pass (PLAN-MOTION.md) adds rules 26 to 31. The numbers run on from section 8 so no older rule is renumbered.

26. Press (review): down is instant, up is eased. `parts/Press` puts `data-down` on the control under the thumb at pointerdown; its down state has no transition, and it comes back over `--t-ui`. Nothing waits for the click to answer a touch.
27. Drawn, not shown (review): a mark that appears is drawn in the order a pen would draw it, over `--t-draw`: the check, the ring round the playing album, the mark under the tab. A mark that goes is lifted over `--t-ui`.
28. The same thing stays the same thing (review): when a thing on one screen is the same thing on the next, it travels there instead of vanishing and appearing: a tapped cover to the small cover by the ♪, a flow's small drawing to the drawing on its page, a playlist's strip to its grid. Travel uses `--ease-settle` and goes through `parts/Turn` (one view transition at a time), so where the browser has none, or Reduce Motion is on, the change just happens.
29. The paper stays, the ink turns (review): between pages, the header's left words, the rules and the tab bar do not move; only what is written moves, a short way, toward the side it came from. Still spreads from the moon in a circle and closes back into the ring; sunrise and sunset stay rule 12's one breath. Sitting is not a turn: it is the one place the paper itself leaves, the header, the rules, the tab bar and every word fading over `--t-light` until only Meditate's circle is left, and coming back the same way.
30. The stream (review): a flow's drawing runs like water, behind `parts/Cycle`. Each step has a pace; the same motes pass every point, so they thin out where it runs fast and pool where it runs slow. With the dot on a step, what is behind it fades; a ring goes out where the dot lands; a held thumb slows all of it on the out-breath. The water is ink, except on Energize (PLAN-ENERGIZE.md X2): there the channel runs as three lanes side by side, one a fixation, each in that fixation's hue in slot order (an empty slot's lane stays ink, and so does all of it in Still and through the door), and a fixation landing in its slot sends the ring out in its hue. `--stream` scales it by the light (Day 1, Night .85, Still .4, Reduce Motion 0). It is a drawing, never a count (rule 15), and the fastest water stays slower than a finger.
31. Motion answers, it never asks (review): every animation starts from a touch or from the sky, runs once, and ends still, except the three that breathe (the ring, with Meditate's circle, which is the ring at the size of the page; the halo; the stream) and the bead on the playing album. Nothing loops to draw the eye, nothing delays a tap, and no screen is unusable while something is moving.

PLAN-ENERGIZE.md adds rule 32, numbered on from the motion pass for the same reason.

32. Energize is the loud page (review): the one screen meant to wind him up, so it turns up what the standard already has and nothing more. Colour: each fixation's hue on its card (a voice's 3px rule and wash, rules 3 and 9) and in its lane of the water (rule 30). Size: the names at `--fs-manifesto`, the largest type in the app, each over a stroke drawn like a pen (rule 27). Speed: its water is already moving at rest (`model/pace.ts`). Bounce, spring and overshoot (rule 18), confetti, badges and streak words (rule 15), anything that loops or asks for attention (rule 31) and any generated word (rule 16) stay banned here as everywhere; every name on it is his.

## 7. Network (lint)

22. The app makes no network request after the first load. `fetch(` is allowed only under `src/music/` (the Spotify tie-in, opt-in, PKCE, no server). Covers are cached as blobs so the album grid works offline.

## 8. Process (review)

23. Every package PR carries its walkthrough step(s) in `design/walkthrough.html`, built from the real components and `tokens.css`, passing `node ~/.claude/hooks/artifact-lint.mjs`.
24. A new screen is reviewed in all three lights before merge (the walkthrough's relight control exists for this).
25. New tokens go into `tokens.css` with a comment naming the screen that needed them; a new colour needs a reason the existing ones do not cover.

## 9. Research behind the choices

- Calm technology (Amber Case): the smallest possible attention, use the periphery, work when it fails → the sky in the periphery, no notifications, offline always. [Calm principles as cited](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.05741), [Designing calm](https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2025/05/designing-calm-ux-principles-for-reducing-users-anxiety.php).
- Slow technology (Hallnäs and Redström; Odom et al. 2022: temporal interconnectedness, temporal drift, explicit slowness) → the page lit by the real sun, the breath, 400ms page turns. [Slow technology](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1007/PL00000019), [office installation study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.11772).
- Self-distancing (Kross and Ayduk): reflecting from a distanced perspective and non-first-person self-talk lowers rumination over time → Turn to answer, the wiser second person. [Chapter](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/emotion-selfcontrol-psych/wp-content/uploads/sites/1322/2017/09/Self-distancing-theory-research-future.pdf), [Kross 2011](https://rascl.studentorg.berkeley.edu/assets/files/kross_ayduk_2011_cd.pdf).
- Lighting and arousal: higher illuminance raises self-rated arousal, colour temperature does not significantly → Still dims rather than warms. [VR lighting study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.10969). Dark vs light reading: no comprehension difference, less glare in low light → Night by sky is safe. [Summary](https://android.gadgethacks.com/news/dark-mode-vs-light-mode-what-research-says-about-when-each-works-best/).
- Apple Journal reviews: restraint works, surfacing what already happened beats prompting, long prompts feel heavy under stress → no prompts, the echo, Still never asks. [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/07/17/how-to-make-apple-journal-part-of-a-mindful-daily-routine), [14-day test](https://blog.vividiary.live/reviews/ios-27-apple-journal-insights-tab-review).
- Web haptics on iPhone: `navigator.vibrate` never shipped in WebKit; the switch trick fires the Taptic Engine on a direct tap, iOS 17.4+, single tick from 26.5. [haptics library notes](https://haptics.kushagragolash.dev/), [state of web haptics 2026](https://creativealive.com/haptics-web-whats-possible-whats-fake-2026/).
- Spotify from a PWA: Web Playback SDK support on iOS Safari is contradictory in the field and stops in the background; the Web API player endpoints need Premium; development mode allows the owner plus a few users. [Grimoire issue](https://github.com/Larront/grimoire/issues/243), [community thread](https://community.spotify.com/t5/Spotify-for-Developers/Web-Playback-SDK-should-work-on-iOS-13-4-but-it-does-not/td-p/5232605).
